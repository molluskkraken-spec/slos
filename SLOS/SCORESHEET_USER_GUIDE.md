# Scoresheet System - Quick Reference Guide

## For Teachers

### Accessing Scoresheet
1. Click "Students" on dashboard
2. Select a student from the list
3. System automatically opens scoresheet for first subject you teach

### Adding Assessment Items
1. Click **"+ Add Item"** button in the category section
2. A new empty row appears
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Title:** Enter assessment name
   - **Score:** Enter points earned (auto-validates max)
   - **Date Posted:** When you assigned the work
   - **Deadline:** When students must submit
4. Status automatically updates based on deadline and score

### Editing Items
1. Click on any field to edit
2. Changes save automatically on blur
3. Table refreshes with latest status

### Deleting Items
1. Click trash icon (<i class="fas fa-trash"></i>)
2. Item is removed and saved

### Switching Quarters/Categories
1. **Quarter Button:** Choose 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th quarter (each has separate data)
2. **Category Button:** Filter by assessment type
3. Only one category displayed at a time for clarity

### Score Entry
- **Concept Notes:** 0-10 points
- **Activities:** 0-100 points
- **Quizzes:** 0-100 points
- **Preliminary Exam:** 0-50 points
- **Departmental Exam:** 0-50 points

### Status Indicators
| Status | Color | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| ✓ Completed | Green | Score entered |
| ✗ Overdue | Red | Deadline passed, no score |
| Pending | Grey | Not yet due or no deadline |

## For Students

### Accessing Your Scoresheet
1. Click "Subjects" on dashboard
2. Click on any subject
3. Your read-only scoresheet opens

### Reading Your Scoresheet
- **Item #:** C1, A2, Q3 (Category + number)
- **Title:** Assessment name
- **Score:** Your earned score / maximum possible
- **Date Posted:** When teacher assigned it
- **Deadline:** Submission deadline
- **Status:** Color-coded completion status

### Filtering Your View
1. **Quarter Button:** Switch between quarters (separately graded periods)
2. **Category Button:** View specific assessment type:
   - Concept Notes (10 pts each)
   - Activities (100 pts each)
   - Quizzes (100 pts each)
   - Preliminary Examination (50 pts)
   - Departmental Examination (50 pts)

### Understanding Status Colors
- **Green (✓ Completed):** Teacher posted your score
- **Red (✗ Overdue):** Deadline passed and not yet graded
- **Grey (Pending):** Waiting for deadline or grading

### Important Notes
- You cannot edit any scoresheet data
- Your scoresheet updates automatically when teacher adds items
- You can view data from all quarters and categories
- Contact your teacher if you see discrepancies

## Troubleshooting

### "Student Not Found" Error
- **Teacher:** Make sure student is enrolled in your subject
- **Student:** Contact administrator to verify enrollment

### "Access Denied" Error
- **Teacher:** Verify you're assigned to this subject
- **Student:** Verify you're enrolled in this subject

### Scores Not Saving
- Check internet connection (if using cloud)
- Verify browser allows localStorage
- Try refreshing page and entering again

### Status Not Updating
- Refresh the page
- Check that date is in YYYY-MM-DD format
- Verify deadline date is correct

### Can't See Student Scoresheet
- Make sure you're logged in as a student
- Verify you're enrolled in the subject
- Check that teacher has posted at least one item

## Category Definitions

### Concept Notes (10 points)
Assessments focused on understanding key concepts and theories
- Examples: Conceptual quizzes, knowledge checks, definitions

### Activities (100 points)
Hands-on tasks and practical exercises
- Examples: Worksheets, group projects, exercises

### Quizzes (100 points)
Formal assessments of topic mastery
- Examples: Unit quizzes, chapter tests, skill checks

### Preliminary Examination (50 points)
First major assessment at end of grading period
- Mid-quarter or mid-semester comprehensive test

### Departmental Examination (50 points)
Final summative assessment
- Final exam, comprehensive test, culminating project

## Score Interpretation

### Perfect Scores
- Concept Notes: 10/10 (100%)
- Activities: 100/100 (100%)
- Quizzes: 100/100 (100%)
- Preliminary: 50/50 (100%)
- Departmental: 50/50 (100%)

### Grade Ranges (Typical)
- 90-100%: Excellent
- 80-89%: Very Good
- 70-79%: Good
- 60-69%: Satisfactory
- Below 60%: Needs Improvement

*Note: Actual grading scale determined by school policy*
